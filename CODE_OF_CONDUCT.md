# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Participation in Granthalay should be welcoming, safe, and respectful. Contributors and
maintainers are expected to act in good faith and give specific, constructive feedback.

## Expected behavior

- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Focus criticism on ideas and changes, not people.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and help repair harm.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and clearly stated boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, publishing private information,
impersonation, spam, deliberate disruption, and retaliation are unacceptable.

## Scope and reporting

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and official
project spaces. Report conduct concerns privately through a contact listed on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/SamsterZero). Do not publish sensitive personal
information in an issue. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect the
community.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/).
